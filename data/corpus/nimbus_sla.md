# NimbusDesk SLA Rules

SLA policies can target first response time, next response time, and resolution
time. Team plan workspaces can create five SLA policies. Scale plan workspaces
can create unlimited SLA policies.

Business hours can be configured per support region. Holidays pause SLA timers
when they are attached to the relevant support calendar.

Priority labels are Low, Normal, High, and Urgent.

Metadata: product=nimbusdesk, doc_type=sla, plan=all
