# NimbusDesk Platform Overview

NimbusDesk is a fictional customer service SaaS product for subscription
businesses. It provides shared inboxes, help center publishing, customer health
signals, and automated response suggestions.

NimbusDesk has two plans:

- Team costs USD 39 per agent per month.
- Scale costs USD 99 per agent per month and includes advanced reporting,
  custom objects, sandbox testing, and SAML SSO.

Help center articles can be drafted in Markdown and published in twelve
languages. The default article revision history is 90 days.
