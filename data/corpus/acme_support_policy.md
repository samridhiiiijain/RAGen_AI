# AcmeOps Support Policy

Starter customers receive email support with a two-business-day first response
target. Growth customers receive email and chat support with an eight-business-hour
first response target.

Enterprise customers receive priority support with a one-hour target for SEV-1
cases and a four-hour target for SEV-2 cases. Enterprise customers also receive
quarterly architecture reviews.

AcmeOps does not provide phone support on the Starter plan.
