Data Exports

NimbusDesk supports CSV exports for tickets, conversations, help center articles, and agent performance reports.

Team plan exports can cover up to 31 days per job. Scale plan exports can cover up to 366 days per job. Export files are retained for 14 days.

Only Admins and Owners can run exports. Export activity appears in audit logs within ten minutes.
