Incident Workflows

AcmeOps can create incidents from email, API calls, manual entry, or alert rules. Each incident receives a severity from SEV-1 to SEV-4.

SEV-1 incidents page the on-call owner immediately. SEV-2 incidents notify the dispatcher queue within five minutes. SEV-3 and SEV-4 incidents are grouped into the standard operations inbox.

Every incident has an owner, status, due time, customer impact field, and postmortem link. Closed SEV-1 incidents require a postmortem within five business days.
