# AcmeOps Platform Overview

AcmeOps is a fictional SaaS operations platform for mid-market logistics teams.
The platform combines ticket routing, fleet exception tracking, customer alerts,
and executive dashboards in a single workspace.

The product has three plans:

- Starter costs USD 49 per active workspace per month and includes 10 users.
- Growth costs USD 149 per active workspace per month and includes 50 users.
- Enterprise starts at USD 499 per active workspace per month and includes SSO,
  private deployment options, and priority support.

AcmeOps stores customer operational events for 18 months by default. Enterprise
customers can extend event retention to 60 months.
