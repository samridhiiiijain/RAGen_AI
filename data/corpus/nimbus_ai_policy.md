# NimbusDesk AI Policy

NimbusDesk response suggestions use the customer's connected knowledge base and
recent ticket history. The AI suggestion feature is disabled by default for new
workspaces and must be enabled by an Admin.

The system does not train vendor foundation models on customer tickets. Customers
can opt out of AI logging by contacting support.

AI confidence labels are Low, Medium, and High. Agents must review suggestions
before sending them to customers.
